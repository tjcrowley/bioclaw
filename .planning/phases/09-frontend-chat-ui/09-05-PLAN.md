  □ Dark background visible (near-black, not white/grey)
  □ Sidebar on the left (~240px); main chat panel on the right

  □ Ask a question in the composer and press Send

  □ The agent's answer appears below it after the response completes

  □ While the agent is working, a tool-call activity panel appears

  □ The activity panel disappears when the answer arrives

  □ The agent's answer contains citation buttons (not raw [ref:…] text)
  □ Clicking a citation button opens a modal/overlay with the raw

  □ The left sidebar lists past sessions (may be empty on first run)
  □ Clicking a past session clears the thread and shows a "Resuming…"
